Installing Sleeve
====================

1. Drag Sleeve into the Applications folder.
2. Open Sleeve from Applications.

The first time, macOS says it can't verify the developer, because this build isn't
notarized by Apple. To open it anyway:

  • macOS 15 and later: click Done, then open System Settings › Privacy & Security,
    scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to the Sleeve message.
  • macOS 14: Control-click Sleeve in Applications, choose Open, then click Open.

You only need to do this once.

Permissions Sleeve may ask for:
  • Control Spotify / Music / your browser: to show what's playing and power the buttons.
  • System audio recording: to make the edge glow react to music. Audio is analysed in
    memory only and is never recorded, saved or uploaded.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
