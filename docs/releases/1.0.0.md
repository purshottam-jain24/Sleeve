# Sleeve 1.0.0

The first public release of Sleeve, a native macOS companion that turns your music into ambient light and a cinematic Now Playing screen.

## Highlights

- **Edge Glow.** Soft, layered light around your display that moves with the music and takes its colours from the album artwork. It includes 8 presets (Ambient, Music Sync, Flow, Pulse, Spectrum, Cinematic, Aurora and Comet) and a studio with live previews. It handles the notch and works across multiple displays, and switches itself off when nothing is playing.
- **Now Playing.** Large artwork over a backdrop coloured by the album, with full playback controls.
- **Synced lyrics.** Three styles (Focus, Karaoke, Glow). Click any line to jump to it, adjust timing per song, search for the right lyrics, and translate on-device (macOS 15+).
- **Works with your players.** Spotify, Apple Music, and music in Safari, Chrome, Arc, Brave, Edge and Vivaldi, including YouTube cover art.
- **Light on battery.** Uses almost no CPU while idle, and only listens to system audio while music is playing. Audio is never recorded or uploaded.

## Install

Download **Sleeve-1.0.0.dmg** below, open it, and drag Sleeve into Applications.

This build isn't notarized, so the first time you open it, macOS asks you to confirm:

- **macOS 15 and later:** System Settings › Privacy & Security › **Open Anyway**
- **macOS 14:** Control-click Sleeve in Applications › **Open**

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.

## Known limitations

- Firefox isn't supported for browser playback, because it doesn't expose tabs to other apps on macOS.
- The glow can't appear over the login screen, Mission Control, or games that take exclusive control of a display.
- Intel Macs are supported, but this release was tested on Apple Silicon only.
